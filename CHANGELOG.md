# MPC Maid Updates

## Next Update

- Redesigned automatic Community Productions as generic 3some castings whose
  MFM, FMF, or FFF type is determined by the three members who join.
- Waiting cast members remain free for normal scenes; the first scene to
  atomically reserve a performer wins and removes them from the waiting cast.
- Shortened productions to five parts over at most 28 minutes and rebalanced
  guaranteed rewards to 90-140 coins and 25-35 XP per performer.
- Added restart revalidation, dynamic remaining-role presentation, and
  race-safe all-member reservation while preserving existing bonus drops and
  exactly-once rewards.
